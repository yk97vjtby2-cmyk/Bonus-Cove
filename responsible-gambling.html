Responsible Gambling

Gambling should be entertainment, not a way to earn money.

Safer gambling

  • Set a spending limit before you start.
  • Set time limits and take regular breaks.
  • Never chase losses.
  • Do not gamble with money needed for essential expenses.
  • Stop and seek support if gambling is causing harm.
